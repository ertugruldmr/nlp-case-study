REAL ERROR 01 · DIGIT SUBSTITUTION

77.543.097 → 77.943.097

Stoklar→engine disagreement+parent sum +400,000→flag; cap 0.4

A plausible 5→9 substitution is caught twice. The pipeline exposes both readings and the violated accounting identity.

REAL ERROR 02 · SEPARATOR CORRUPTION

4.224 → 4,224

financial investments→engines agree+impossible .224 residue→parent sum

Agreement cannot catch a shared interpretation error. Financial consistency localises the residual while cell confidence remains 0.85.

Deep probes

One-minute judgment answers

Open an artifact only when the interviewer requests proof or implementation depth.

Why not one VLM or whole-document LLM call?

DECISION: staged contract. EVIDENCE: provenance, number states, confidence and checks fail differently; measured Qwen structure is 187/201 versus shipped 199/201. LIMIT: this scan/setup. NEXT: VLM behind the same schema/checks.

Why model plus rules?

DECISION: semantic ranker plus value/role reconciliation. EVIDENCE: labels-only 1/7 vs labels+values 7/7. LIMIT: seven links. NEXT: held-out semantic/reconciliation slices.

Is 0.93 a 93% correctness probability?

DECISION: no; document-scoped review priority. EVIDENCE: 33 controls, fitted Platt, LOO Brier 0.0085 and interval. LIMIT: small same-document controls. NEXT: held-out calibration.

What does the full-stack app add?

DECISION: expose alternatives, provenance and review behavior. EVIDENCE: 18 scenarios, API, walkthrough, triage, labels/export; 79 app tests and GREEN preflight. LIMIT: defense lab. NEXT: durable jobs, auth, audit and SLOs.

What would you improve first?

DECISION: evidence breadth before complexity. EVIDENCE: one-file strength and known cross-filing gaps. LIMIT: no production-labelled distribution. NEXT: annotation protocol and document holdouts.
